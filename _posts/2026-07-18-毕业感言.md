## Time to Say Goodbye: My Middle School Graduation Reflection

Standing here today, looking back at the past three years, my heart is filled with a mixture of joy and worry. We arrived here as kids, and we are leaving as young adults. **How quickly time flies!** It feels like just yesterday we walked through these school gates for the very first time, full of curiosity and innocence. Yet, in the blink of an eye, three wonderful years have slipped through our fingers, leaving us standing at the crossroads of our youth.

### To My Homeroom and English Teacher, Miss Qi

To begin with, I want to express my deepest gratitude to **Miss Qi**. As my English teacher and homeroom teacher, you have been my anchor. Thank you for not only helping me improve my English but also for looking after me in my daily life. Your constant support and care made our classroom feel like a second home, and I will carry your lessons with me forever.

### To My Physics Teacher, Teacher Xu

To **Teacher Xu**, thank you for your unwavering belief in me. There were "thousands of times" when my physics grades were so bad that I wanted to give up on myself, but you never did. Your trust and encouragement during my lowest moments gave me the confidence to keep trying. Thank you for believing in me when I felt like I couldn't keep going.

### To My Subject Teachers: Thank You for Shaping My World

I am also grateful to the other wonderful teachers who made my middle school years so colorful:

- **Teacher Huang (History):** Thank you for your excellent guidance and leadership. You turned history from a list of dry dates into a fascinating story, inspiring me to look at the world through a wider view.
    
- **Teacher Luo (Biology):** Thank you for your dedicated education over the past two years. You opened the door to the wonders of science and life for us.
    
- **Teacher Tian (IT):** Thank you for your engaging explanations of computer technology, equipping us with the digital skills we need for the future.
    

In addition, standing at the finish line of this journey, I am filled with a sense of sad for our middle school days. These three years have been a beautiful tapestry woven with laughter, tears, sweat, and unforgettable friendships. We ran together on the sports field, stay up late before big exams, and shared countless jokes during class breaks. Every corner of this campus holds a piece of our youth, and the thought of leaving it behind brings a bittersweet ache to my heart. And I just can not believe I am graduated from my middle school.

### Moving Forward

As we turn the page to the next chapter of our lives, the memories of these classrooms, the laughter of my classmates, and the wisdom of my teachers will remain my greatest treasures. Thank you, my dear teachers, for molding us into who we are today. We will make you proud!
